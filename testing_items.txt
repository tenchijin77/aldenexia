== WHOLE-GAME COMBAT CHANGES ==
[ ] Melee abilities cast instantly (Power Strike, Backstab, Holy Strike, the Aetherfist strikes, taunts).
[ ] Moving during a spell cast fizzles it ("You moved, and your spell fizzles."); turning in place doesn't. Troubadour songs
    keep playing while moving.
[ ] Knockback: Power Strike / Earth Strike push the monster ~4m away; it walks back and fights. Works on the server too.
[ ] Slows, curses, stuns and fears no longer do damage by themselves.
[ ] Group heals heal everyone nearby (Holy Light, Prayer of Healing, Wild Growth, Tranquility, Aetherfist Meditation /
    Mend Wounds) — test in a group.
[ ] Area spells hit monsters near the target (Fireball, Hurricane, Plague Cloud); cones and point-blank spells hit the
    right radius.
[ ] Drains heal YOU (Life Drain, Death Coil, Harmony Blade, Siphon Essence on a monster).
[ ] Chance effects happen only sometimes (Smite's blind ~10%).
[ ] Blink (10m) and Chaos Rift (8m, random direction) teleport.
[ ] Attacking breaks Vanish / Shadow Cloak / Camouflage / Invisibility.
[ ] Level-1 openers no longer one-shot a level-1 monster (Power Strike 35, Aimed Shot 40, Backstab 60); Shadow Bolt and
    Chaos Bolt (45) feel useful.
