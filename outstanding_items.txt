== SPELLS AND ABILITIES ==
- Multi-target riders are simplified: "chains to 1 enemy" (Master Ki Strike, Volatile Spark, Master Riposte), "affects N
  allies" (these reach everyone in range instead), "spreads to 2 enemies" (Plague Wave), "hits 2 enemies" (Improved
  Shatter uses a 3 m splash), Mass Charm / Improved Mesmerize / Polymorphic Surge extra targets.
- Summons that describe several animals give one pet (one pet at a time): Verdant Call (2), Wild Call (2), Wild Legion (3).
- Summon extras not modelled: wraith accuracy/damage debuff aura, shade mana drain, lifesteal (Eternal Wraith, Eternal
  Abomination), hawk "scouting".
- Not built: spell reflection (Spell Ward, Prismatic Veil — monsters don't cast yet), shields that explode when they break
  (Wild Barrier, Entropic Ward/Aegis), Nights Embrace (reset cooldowns), resurrection (Resurrection, Improved Resurrection,
  Ancestral Revival, Improved Ancestral Revival), execute conditions (Assassinate, Soul Reaper "below 20% health"),
  Quivering Palm instant kill, Void Strike mana drain, Deadly Aim armour ignore, Ice Lance +20% vs slowed targets,
  Aimed Shot / Shadow Bolt / Chaos Bolt crit riders, Arcane Flux backfire, Chaos Weave mana penalty, charm/polymorph
  "success rate" bonuses, Thorn Whip's pull, Radiant/Healing "chain" heals, Symphony/Crescendo's ally buff half,
  Wild Surge "random effect" variety, Soul Harvest (targets corpses; corpse targeting isn't supported).
- Monster spellcasting doesn't exist yet. When it's built: casts break when the monster moves (knockback already cancels
  one), Enhanced Block's 10% magic reduction and spell reflection start to matter.
- Perfect Balance / Master Balance knockback immunity: nothing knocks players back yet.
- Death Pact spends its mana and recast even when there is no pet to sacrifice (it says so); a pre-check before the cast
  would refund it.
- 365 spells have no scroll (mostly levels 9-20). Upgrades have scrolls; base high-level spells mostly don't.
- Data/player_spells.xlsx is stale (last saved 2026-09-16); Data/player_spells.json is the source of truth.
- Pickpocket's "already picked" mark is per player on their own machine (resets if the monster respawns — intended).
- Verify: the weapon poison coating (Session 50 note) "never wears off and a second application does not stack".
