STANCE SYSTEM

All stances have a 10 second cooldown to switch between them and persist until changed. Only one stance can be active at a time. Hover over them to see their details, and show in the spell book as well so people can see the details there if desired. They should be automatically unlocked, with no need to learn them.

Shadow Ascendant (Offensive Stance)
Effect: +15% damage, -10% defense, -35% threat generation
Use case: DPS or solo play, not recommended for group tanking

Necrotic Bastion (Defensive Stance)
Effect: +15% defense, -10% damage
Unique Bonus: +25% Life Drain healing
This stance offsets the damage loss through enhanced life steal and maintains threat generation through healing, making it ideal for group tanking situations.

Dusk Walker (Balanced Stance)
Effect: +7% damage, +7% defense
Use case: Flexible for mixed scenarios, solo or group content

CORE MECHANICS

Life Drain Sustainability: Life-draining attacks heal the Voidknight for a portion of damage dealt, ensuring survivability increases with combat intensity.

Undead Army: Voidknights summon skeleton warriors, wraiths, and shades that fight alongside them. These pets have no duration and persist until killed, allowing players to build an army of undead allies.

Necromantic Control: DoTs and debuffs are Voidknight's primary tool for controlling enemy behavior and weakening them over time.

Resource System: Mana (regenerates passively)

PET SYSTEM

All Voidknight summons follow these rules:
- No duration - persist until killed by enemy damage
- Inherit player buffs and debuffs normally (player and group can cast buffs on them)
- Auto-defend player when attacked
- Can be resummoned after death
- Follow standard commands: Attack, Follow, Guard, Passive

MOB Appraisal system (this lets the player see monster difficulty, weaknesses, and faction)
HYBRID APPRAISAL SYSTEM

AUTOMATIC INFO (always shown):
- NPC Name
- Level (colored by difficulty: Green/Yellow/Orange/Red/Purple)
- Basic faction standing (Ally/Neutral/Enemy) Nameplate color: Ally: Blue, Neutral: White, Enemy: Red

INSIGHT CHECK (skill-based, cooldown):
- Press C or use Appraise command /appraise
- Roll Check (Intelligence or Wisdom, whichever is higher)

APPRAISAL ROLL MECHANIC:

Roll vs. target's Defense (Intelligence or Wisdom, whichever is higher)

DC (Difficulty Class) by target level difference:

Green (5+ levels below): DC 5 (auto-success)
Yellow (2-4 levels below): DC 10 (easy)
Orange (Equal level): DC 15 (moderate)
Red (2-4 levels above): DC 20 (hard)
Purple (5+ levels above): DC 25 (very hard)

ROLL FORMULA:
Player rolls d20 + (Intelligence modifier OR Wisdom modifier, whichever is higher) vs. Target DC

SUCCESS:

Display all difficulty text + faction modifiers
Reveal resistances, weaknesses, special abilities
Show loot quality estimate
Show exact faction reputation

FAILURE:

Display only base difficulty color (might be wrong)
Show generic "You sense nothing unusual"
Color could flash incorrectly then correct after 10 seconds
No additional details revealed

CRITICAL SUCCESS (roll 20):

All success info PLUS hidden details (secret boss mechanic, rare loot chance, etc.)

CRITICAL FAILURE (roll 1):

Difficulty color is completely wrong for 30 seconds
Misleading text (appears weaker/stronger than actual)
Then corrects naturally

COOLDOWN: 10 seconds between Appraisals on same target
- Success reveals:
  - Exact difficulty assessment text
  - Estimated threat level
  - Specific faction modifiers
  - Special notes (weaknesses, resistances, loot quality)
- Failure reveals:
  - Generic "You sense nothing unusual" or misread the threat level
  - Could display wrong difficulty color temporarily
- Cooldown: 10 seconds per target (prevents spam)



COLOR CODING:
- Green = Trivial (low XP)
- Yellow = Easy (standard)
- Orange = Moderate (challenging)
- Red = Hard (dangerous)
- Purple = Deadly (impossible)

FACTION MODIFIERS:
- Allies get friendly text
- Enemies get hostile warnings
- Neutrals get cautious assessment
